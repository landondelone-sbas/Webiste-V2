About us

A compliance partner, not a report vendor.

SBAS Info LLC was built around a simple observation: most practices don't fail HIPAA because they're careless. They fail because compliance is fragmented across vendors, and nobody owns the whole picture.

Our mission

To make HIPAA compliance something a practice manager can hold in one hand — a single evidence file, current and defensible, produced on request without a scramble.

We think the industry has that backwards. It sells assessments as products, when what practices need is continuity: the same people who found the gap staying around to help close it, and remembering your environment the following year.

That's the whole thesis of the business. Everything else — the scoping, the training, the site visit rehearsals — follows from it.

How we work

  • Plain language, always

    Findings are written so a practice manager can act on them without a security background. If we can't explain it simply, we don't understand it well enough yet.

  • Scoped honestly

    We quote to your actual footprint. A two-provider clinic is not billed like a multi-site group, and we'll tell you when you don't need something.

  • Minimal clinical disruption

    Interviews and walkthroughs are scheduled around patient hours. Your schedule is the constraint; we plan around it.

  • We stay after the report

    Remediation questions don't stop when the PDF is delivered, so neither do we.

Questions we get first

Do we actually have to do a Security Risk Analysis?

Yes. The HIPAA Security Rule requires every covered entity and business associate to conduct an accurate and thorough assessment of risks to electronic protected health information, and to review and update it as conditions change. It is not a one-time filing.

What does it cost?

We don't publish a rate card, because scope genuinely varies with practice size, number of locations, and systems in use. You get a fixed written quote after the scoping call, and you pay that amount — no hourly overruns.

Will you have access to patient data?

We scope engagements to avoid handling PHI wherever possible — we assess the controls around your data, not the data itself. Where incidental access is unavoidable, we execute a Business Associate Agreement first.

What's the difference between a project and a retainer?

A project is a one-time scope — an assessment, a training rollout, a site visit prep — paid once. A retainer is a monthly arrangement where we keep your evidence file current year-round and stay available for questions. Both are paid on the payment page.
